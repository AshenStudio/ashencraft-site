Bedrock Edition

AshenCraft isn't available on Minecraft: Bedrock Edition. However, if you play Minecraft Bedrock on a computer, you likely already own Minecraft: Java Edition as well! The two editions often come bundled together, so check your library or the Minecraft launcher before buying anything.

Once you have Java Edition installed, open the AshenCraft launcher: it installs the modpack, signs you in and gets you straight into the world of Ashen.
