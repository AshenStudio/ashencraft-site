AshenCraft

A handcrafted fantasy MMORPG built on Minecraft

Pick a weapon, master its skills, and fight across a living world. Everything runs through the AshenCraft launcher: sign in, install, and play.

Weapon-based skills

Six weapon families, each with its own active skills, passives and a defense move. Your weapon defines your style.

Monsters and bosses

Leveled mobs across the world. Contribute to a boss kill and earn loot by how much you fought.

Waystone network

Discover steles, light them, and travel the world through paid teleports. Every published waystone appears on the live map.

A real economy

Silver and Ashens wallets, player-to-player payments, and gear worth collecting.

Parties

Group with up to four heroes, share the fight, and lean on each other in battle.

A cinematic world

Custom models, particle effects and camera sequences for the moments that matter.
